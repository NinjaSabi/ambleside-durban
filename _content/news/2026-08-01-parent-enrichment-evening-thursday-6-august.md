**Parent Enrichment Evening: Parenting with Loving Authority**

What is the answer when your child asks, “Why must I?”

Many of us know the answer we grew up hearing: “Because I said so!” Yet most parents recognise that this response, while sometimes tempting, does not fully address the deeper questions our children are asking.

Should our children listen to us? If so, why?

**Join us on Thursday, 6 August, as Jean Louw, Director of Ambleside South Africa, explores what it means to Parent with Loving Authority.**

As parents, we hold a God-given authority entrusted to us for the flourishing of our children. What does it mean to steward that authority wisely, lovingly, and faithfully? How do we establish healthy boundaries while nurturing strong relationships? And in a culture where children are often encouraged to lead themselves, how do we lovingly protect them from carrying responsibilities that rightly belong to parents?

Jean brings a wealth of experience as a principal at a Christian pre-school, Ambleside teacher, and mother to Alex and Amy. Drawing on biblical principles, Charlotte Mason's philosophy, and years of practical experience, she will help us think deeply about our role as parents and the privilege of shepherding our children well.

At Ambleside Durban, our termly Parent Enrichment Evenings are designed to strengthen the partnership between home and school, encourage meaningful conversations, and provide a welcoming space for parents to grow together. We are delighted to open this event to parents and families from the wider community as we continue navigating the joys and challenges of parenting together.

We look forward to welcoming you for an evening of encouragement, wisdom, and practical insight.
