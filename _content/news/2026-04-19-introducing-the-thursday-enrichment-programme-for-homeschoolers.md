One of the questions we hear most often from homeschooling families is this: "Where can my child experience structured, enriching learning alongside other children — without having to commit to full-time school?"

We have been thinking about that question for a while. And our answer is the Thursday Enrichment Programme.

Every Thursday during May, we opened our school to homeschooled children in Grades 1 to 7 from 10:00 to 12:30. The mornings followed a simple, unhurried rhythm: Nature Study outdoors, Composer Study and Music Appreciation, and a shared break for free play.

The programme grew from the heart of what we believe — that learning is richer in community, and that the best things in education are hard to do alone. It was never about replacing what families had built at home, only adding to it.

Those Thursday mornings have come to a close for now, but much of what made them special happens at Ambleside every day. If you are a homeschooling family wondering what school could look like the Charlotte Mason way, we would love to show you around.
