**Update:** this position has now been filled, and we're grateful to everyone who applied. We are still looking for a [Grade 6–7 teacher for 2027](/news/article/?slug=2026-08-17-were-hiring-grade-6-7-teacher-2027). If you're a parent considering Ambleside, you're welcome to [book a school visit](https://cal.com/ambleside/visit) and meet the team.

*Are you a godly, warm, engaging teacher who can shepherd a combined group of  Grade R & 1 students, and inspire joy in discovery, helping our children grow in character & master their learning?*

**Ambleside is a Christian school, so it is vital that our teachers:**

* have a living faith in Jesus
* adopt a mindset of growth for both their students and themselves
* will teach using the Ambleside Schools International curriculum and methodology 

  *(all training and full support is provided)*

**Responsibilities:**

Duties include preparing lessons, monitoring progress, marking work, assessing students and documenting progress, proactively supporting weaknesses, assigning additional work where necessary, maintaining a positive alliance with parents, and managing the classroom area, as well as participation in school life and events.

The role is a full-time teaching post, 7am-3pm Monday to Friday during term time, plus ad hoc school events, extra curricular activities as needed, shared holiday club responsibilities, training, and preparation times as necessary.

***If this sounds like a wonderful opportunity to invest in the lives of our children and our Ambleside Family, please send the following to admin@amblesidedurban.com:***

*1) Curriculum Vitae*

*2) Letter of Motivation to support your application*

*3) a Statement of Faith* 

Candidates who qualify for the first round of interviews will be contacted.
